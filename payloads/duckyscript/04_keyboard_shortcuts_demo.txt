REM ============================================================
REM  04 - iPadOS Keyboard Shortcut Demo
REM  Target : iPad Air / iPadOS 26 (O.MG Cable Elite as keyboard)
REM  Goal   : Practice the GUI (Command) modifier and see which
REM           iPadOS system shortcuts a keyboard can drive. This is
REM           purely navigation - it moves you around the UI and
REM           returns you home. Nothing is created or changed.
REM  Setup  : Start at the Home screen. Watch the screen as it runs.
REM ============================================================

DELAY 2000

REM --- Spotlight ---
REM GUI SPACE opens Spotlight search. ESCAPE (or GUI SPACE again) closes it.
GUI SPACE
DELAY 1000
ESCAPE
DELAY 1000

REM --- App switcher ---
REM GUI TAB opens the app switcher; release returns to the highlighted app.
REM (Hold behavior varies by firmware; this is a quick tap-through.)
GUI TAB
DELAY 1200

REM --- Go Home ---
REM GUI H acts like pressing the Home gesture, returning to the Home screen.
GUI h
DELAY 1000

REM --- Show all shortcuts ---
REM Press and hold GUI on iPadOS normally shows the shortcut cheat-sheet.
REM A quick tap here just re-focuses; use this as a placeholder to
REM experiment with your own combos, e.g.:
REM   GUI SHIFT 3   (screenshot on some setups)
REM   GUI SHIFT h   (varies)
REM Try one at a time and observe.

REM End back on the Home screen.
GUI h
